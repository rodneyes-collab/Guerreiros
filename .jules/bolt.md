## 2025-02-28 - Unnecessary Rerenders via Zustand Destructuring\n**Learning:** The `MatchView.tsx` component was destructuring the entire `useStore` Zustand hook, causing it to re-render whenever ANY store variable changed. A major culprit was `elapsedTime` which changes very frequently (e.g. every second). Zustand recommends atomic selectors or `useShallow` for performance.\n**Action:** Replaced large destructuring with `useShallow` object selections and direct atomic selectors (`useStore(state => state.property)`) to drastically reduce unnecessary re-renders in highly active components.
